# Gold Layer

## Objective

The Gold Layer delivers business-ready data products for executive dashboards, workforce intelligence, predictive modeling and GenAI applications.

This layer translates cleaned workforce data into actionable decision-support assets.

---

## Data Products

### executive_scorecard

Purpose:
Provide high-level workforce KPIs for leadership.

Main metrics:
- headcount
- resignation_rate
- average_performance_score
- average_satisfaction_score
- average_salary
- average_overtime_hours

---

### workforce_intelligence

Purpose:
Analyze workforce productivity, workload and development.

Main metrics:
- workforce_load_index
- workforce_efficiency_index
- growth_index
- training_hours
- projects_handled
- promotion_count

---

### burnout_monitoring

Purpose:
Identify teams or employees with potential burnout signals.

Main metrics:
- overtime_hours
- work_hours_per_week
- sick_days
- satisfaction_score
- burnout_risk_score
- burnout_risk_category

---

### promotion_readiness

Purpose:
Identify employees with strong development and promotion potential.

Main metrics:
- performance_score
- training_hours
- projects_handled
- years_at_company
- promotion_readiness_score
- promotion_readiness_category

---

### attrition_intelligence

Purpose:
Support attrition prediction and explainable AI analysis.

Main metrics:
- attrition_flag
- monthly_income
- overtime_flag
- job_satisfaction
- work_life_balance
- years_since_last_promotion
- attrition_risk_score

---

### genai_context

Purpose:
Provide curated business context for the GenAI Workforce Advisor.

Main content:
- executive KPI summaries
- department-level risk summaries
- attrition drivers
- burnout insights
- recommended action areas

---

## Design Principles

- Build reusable data products
- Serve dashboards, ML models and AI agents
- Separate business-ready metrics from raw data
- Prioritize explainability and decision-making
